2018–2024 · McDowall Print

From artwork
to finished print.

Design / Digital print / Pre-press / Finishing

I spent seven years working across design, digital printing, pre-press and finishing. That included checking files, preparing dielines, managing colour, proofing and working with clients to get jobs ready for production.

I built a variable-data workflow that generated and printed more than 100,000 unique cards, using Code 128 barcodes and QR codes for a repeat client.

Artwork & pre-press

Print-ready files, bleed, dielines, image preparation, CMYK and spot colour, proofing and production handover.

Print & finishing

Digital print, labels and wide format. Experience with Xerox, Fujifilm, Epson SurePress, Fiery RIP, binding, laminating and guillotines.
